Program Name

Bruce Jones Home Automation Alerts

Program Description

This is a private home automation notification program operated by Bruce Jones for the sole use of Bruce Jones and members of his immediate household. The program sends automated text message alerts triggered by home automation events such as security, environmental, and pet-related conditions. The program is non-commercial and is not offered to the general public.

Eligibility

This program is available only to members of the Bruce Jones household who are 18 years of age or older and who personally consent to receive messages at their own mobile number. Consent is not transferable, and recipients may not enroll a mobile number that does not belong to them.

Opt-In

Authorized household members opt in by visiting the SMS registration page, entering their contact information, and checking the consent box before submitting the form. The registration form states that message frequency varies based on household events, message and data rates may apply, and recipients may reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on home automation events. Recipients may receive anywhere from zero to several messages per day.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Recipients should consult their wireless carrier's plan for details.

Help

For help, recipients may reply HELP to any message or contact the operator at bruce@brucejones.org.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying STOP to any message from the program. After replying STOP, the recipient will receive one confirmation message and no further messages will be sent.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
