Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

This privacy policy describes how the personal home automation notification system ("the System") operated by Bruce Jones handles information.

Scope

The System is a private, non-commercial home automation messaging service used solely by the operator and members of the operator's immediate household. It is not offered to the public.

Information Collected

The System collects only the mobile phone numbers of household members who have explicitly consented to receive messages, and the content of automated alerts generated by household automation events (for example, security, environmental, or pet-related notifications).

How Information Is Used

Phone numbers and message content are used exclusively to deliver home automation alerts to consenting household members. Information is transmitted through Twilio, Inc. for the sole purpose of message delivery.

Sharing

Information collected by the System is not shared with any third party. Information is not used for marketing purposes. Information is not sold, rented, or otherwise disclosed.

Retention

Phone numbers are retained only as long as the recipient remains a household member and consents to receive messages. Message logs are retained only as needed for system troubleshooting.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to: bruce@brucejones.org
