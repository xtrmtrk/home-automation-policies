Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 10, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Bruce Jones Home Automation Alerts ("the System") handles information.

Scope

The System is a private, non-commercial home automation messaging service operated by Bruce Jones and used solely by Bruce Jones and members of his immediate household. It is not offered to the public.

Information Collected

The System collects the name, mobile phone number, email address, consent status, consent text, registration page URL, and registration timestamp for household members who explicitly consent to receive messages. The System also processes the content of automated alerts generated by household automation events, such as security, environmental, or pet-related notifications.

How Information Is Used

Contact information, consent records, and message content are used exclusively to document consent and deliver home automation alerts to consenting household members. Information is transmitted through Twilio, Inc. for the sole purpose of message delivery.

Sharing

Information collected by the System is not shared with any third party. Information is not used for marketing purposes. Information is not sold, rented, or otherwise disclosed.

SMS Consent and Mobile Information

No mobile information, phone numbers, or SMS opt-in data will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories of information described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances. SMS consent is collected solely to enable delivery of Bruce Jones Home Automation Alerts via Twilio, Inc.

Retention

Phone numbers are retained only as long as the recipient remains a household member and consents to receive messages. Message logs are retained only as needed for system troubleshooting.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to: bruce@brucejones.org
